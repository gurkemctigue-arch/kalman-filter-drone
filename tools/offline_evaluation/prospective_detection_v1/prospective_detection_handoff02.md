The prospective rule is first recorded FOLLOW clock +1 second, then every2 seconds through the final recorded clock. Every grid opportunity is retained, including the tail after image/detector/tracker loss. The nearest original source RGB within0.1 second is selected; exact ties choose the earlier source acquisition timestamp. Duplicate source timestamps are ambiguous and no convenient duplicate is selected. There is no45-image cap. Recorded clock regression, missing clock or missing first FOLLOW yields unknown sampling, never a takeoff-time substitute. The retained clock/FOLLOW/RGB metadata index allows the outer verifier to recompute the fixed schedule and nearest/tie/duplicate choices without reading bags again.
